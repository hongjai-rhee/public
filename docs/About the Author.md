# About the Author

## **Hongjai Rhee**
**Professor, Ajou University**  
📧 [hrhee@ajou.ac.kr](mailto:hrhee@ajou.ac.kr)

Hongjai Rhee received his Ph.D. in **Economics from the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)**, where he was advised by **Professor Guido W. Imbens**, a Nobel Laureate in Economic Sciences.

He is currently a Professor at the **School of Business, Ajou University**, where he teaches **Marketing Analytics** and **Big Data Analysis**.

His research interests include **econometric analysis, machine learning, and data analytics**, and he has published numerous academic papers in domestic and international journals. He has also received the **Prime Minister's Commendation** of the Republic of Korea and the **Ajou University Award for Excellence in Teaching** multiple times.
