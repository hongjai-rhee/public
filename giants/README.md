## Ideas do not belong to us in isolation. They live in relationships.

Every model, algorithm, and explanation in this book is connected to ideas developed by others—ideas that were extended, combined, challenged, implemented, and passed forward.

This directory acknowledges some of the people whose intellectual work made this book possible.

I stand on their shoulders, as they stood on the shoulders of those before them.

The goal is not to claim the ideas, but to carry them forward.
